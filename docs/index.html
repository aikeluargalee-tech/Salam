BEGINNER GLOSSARY — 6 TERMS IN 30 SECONDS PLAIN DEFINITIONS
SPOT VS FUTURES: Spot is buying real Bitcoin directly. Futures is betting on price with borrowed money (often moves faster and reverses sharply).
FLOOR & CEILING: A floor (support) is a price area where drops previously bounced. A ceiling (resistance) is a price area where rallies previously stalled.
SWEEP (POKE & BOUNCE): Price briefly pokes past a floor or ceiling and closes right back inside — a sign of trapped traders.
FUNDING RATE: Small fee paid between futures buyers and sellers. High positive means many traders are betting on up moves; negative means many bet down.
LIQUIDATIONS: Forced closes when a trader with borrowed money runs out of margin (10x–100x leverage). Clusters act like magnets for fast wicks.
WHY "WAIT" IS COMMON: Most hours have no valid trigger. Staying patient protects you from buying or selling in the middle of noise.
